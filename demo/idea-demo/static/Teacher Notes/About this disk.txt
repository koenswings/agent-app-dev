IDEA-DEMO disk
The kolibri folder holds three channels (Grade 5 Science, Early Reading, Maths).
When the disk is plugged in, IDEA builds them into Kolibri automatically.
Worksheets and Teacher Notes appear in Nextcloud Files as "USB: IDEA-DEMO".
All files are openly licensed; see CREDITS.txt. The Khan Academy maths videos
are CC BY-NC-SA: use them for non-commercial teaching only.
The Siyavula Grade 5A Learner's Book and Teacher's Guide are CC BY-ND: share
them freely, but do not change them.

Demo sign-in (Kolibri "IDEA Demo School"): admin, teacher1, learner1,
learner2, learner3. Each password is the account name followed by 911!
(for example teacher1911!). Change them before real use.
