§1   The Energy Model

The baby's daily nutritional need is derived from the paediatric guideline of 150 ml of prepared formula per kilogram of body weight per 24 hours. This gives a constant hourly drain rate:

Dtarget = wkg ⁢ r (ml / 24 h) λ = Dtarget 24 (ml / h,  constant) where wkg is the baby's weight in kg and r=150 ml/kg/day (default). For a 6.9 kg baby: Dtarget=1035 ml, λ=43.1 ml/h.

Prepared milk is used as the direct proxy for caloric energy: 1 ml of prepared milk = 1 unit of energy. The standard interval — the time for one preferred bottle to be "spent" at rate λ — is:

SI = mbottle λ (hours) For a 90 ml water / 100 ml prepared milk bottle at 43.1 ml/h: SI≈2h19m.

§2   The 24-Hour Intake Function

Rather than tracking an all-time running balance, the app tracks the total milk received within a rolling 24-hour window. The credit contributed by a bottle of volume mi given at time ti, evaluated at time T, is:

credit T−ti mi = mi if T−ti≤24 h max 0 mi − λ ⁢ T−ti−24 if T−ti>24 h

The total smoothed 24-hour intake at time T is the sum over all feeds logged before T:

intake (T) = ∑ i=1 n credit T−ti mi

The decay rate past 24 h is exactly λ — the same rate at which the baby consumes energy. This choice is not arbitrary: it means that a bottle's contribution to the 24-hour window diminishes at exactly the speed at which its nutritional value is consumed. In steady-state feeding, the net change per interval is zero, and intake oscillates between Dtarget and Dtarget+mbottle.

§3   Predictor A — Adjusted Volume at Standard Time

Predictor A answers: "if I feed at the standard time SI after the last feed, how much should I give?" It solves for the volume that restores intake to the equilibrium peak Dtarget+mbottle.

TA = tlast + SI VA = Dtarget + mbottle − intake (TA) VA is converted to water ml via the inverse of the water→milk table and shown to the parent. At equilibrium: intake(TA)=Dtarget, so VA=mbottle — the standard bottle.

If VA≤0, the baby is still above the equilibrium ceiling at the standard time — Predictor A is greyed out and Predictor B takes over. If VA>mcap, the value is capped at the stomach capacity limit (§5).

§4   Predictor B — Standard Volume at Adjusted Time

Predictor B answers: "when should I give my preferred standard bottle?" It finds the time TB at which the 24-hour intake has decayed back to exactly the daily target, so that adding one standard bottle restores intake to the equilibrium peak:

intake (TB) = Dtarget Feed when the 24-hour intake returns to Dtarget. Adding mbottle then restores intake to Dtarget+mbottle.

TB is found by binary search on the interval [tlast,tlast+Tmax], where Tmax is the search horizon (default 48 h). Three cases:

ConditionResultMeaning
intake(tlast)≤Dtarget TB=tlast Feed now — baby is at or below target
intake(Tmax)>Dtarget TB=Tmax Still in surplus at horizon — cap applies
otherwise binary search until |intake(TB)−Dtarget|<1 ml

At equilibrium TB=tlast+SI — the standard rhythm. With a deficit, TB<tlast+SI; with a surplus, TB>tlast+SI.

§5   Stomach Capacity Constraint

The total undigested milk in the stomach at time T — the milk not yet metabolised — is modelled as:

Sload (T) = ∑ i=1 n max 0 mi − λ ⁢ T−ti

The constraint is that the stomach load immediately after a feed must not exceed the capacity mcap (one standard bottle size up from preferred). This gives a minimum elapsed time before the next feed:

Δtmin = max 0 mlast + mbottle − mcap λ For a 6.9 kg baby with 90 ml water (100 ml milk) preferred bottle and stomach cap of 135 ml: Δtmin = 100+100−13543.1 ≈ 1h 30m

If Predictor B's TB falls before tlast+Δtmin, it is clamped upward with a note: "Stomach not ready — baby is underfed but needs time to digest."
