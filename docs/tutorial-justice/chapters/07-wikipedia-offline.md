## W1. Look something up and use it in class
1. Open Kiwix from the Console (chapter 2). <!-- screenshot:W1-01 -->
2. Click the Wikipedia library card. <!-- screenshot:W1-02 -->
3. Type in the search box, e.g. `Fraction`, and open the article. <!-- screenshot:W1-03 -->
4. Follow blue links to related articles. <!-- screenshot:W1-04 -->
Classroom idea: give learners 3 questions to answer from one article; they read on their own device.
