# 2. Getting connected and opening an app

You need a phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser, and the school's IDEA Wi-Fi.

1. Connect your device to the IDEA Wi-Fi. Ask your IDEA contact for the Wi-Fi name and password.
2. Open the browser and go to the Console address your IDEA contact gives you. The Console opens on the **Apps** page: one card per app, each with a short description and a blue **Open** button. The green dot at the top shows the Console is connected to the IDEA box.
   ![Console Apps page (two of the cards)](../screenshots/CON-01.png)
3. Click the blue **Open** button on the card of the app you need. The app opens in a new tab. Here it is Kolibri's sign-in page.
   ![Kolibri opened from the Console](../screenshots/CON-03.png)

Good to know:

- A card that is greyed out and says **Not available** is not running. Do not try to fix it: tell your school operator.
- Do not type numbers (ports) into the address bar. Always use **Open** in the Console.
- The person icon at the top right (**Operator Login**) is for the operator (Part B). Teachers do not need it.

> **Tip.** You can bookmark the page that opens. If the bookmark stops working, go back to the Console and use **Open** again.
