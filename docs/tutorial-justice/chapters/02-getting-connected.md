# 2. Getting connected and opening an app

You need: a phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser, and the school's IDEA Wi-Fi.

1. Connect your device to the IDEA Wi-Fi (name and password: **to confirm** per school; ask your IDEA contact).
2. Open the browser and go to the Console address your IDEA contact gives you. The Console opens on the **Apps** page: one card per app (Kiwix, Kolibri, Nextcloud, …). The green dot at the top shows the Console is connected to the IDEA box.
   ![Console Apps page](../screenshots/CON-01.png)
3. Find the card of the app you need and click its blue **Open** button. The app opens in a new tab. Here: Kolibri's sign-in page.
   ![Kolibri opened from the Console](../screenshots/CON-03.png)

Important:

- A card that is greyed out and says **Not available** is not running. Do not try to fix it: tell your IDEA contact or the school operator.
- Do not type numbers (ports) into the address bar. Always use **Open** in the Console.
- The Console has an operator area (person icon, **Operator Login**). Teachers do not need it.

Tip: you can bookmark the page that opens. If the bookmark stops working, go back to the Console and use **Open** again.
