# 2. Getting connected and opening an app

You need: a phone, tablet or laptop with a web browser, and the school's IDEA Wi-Fi.

1. Connect your device to the IDEA Wi-Fi (name and password: **to confirm** per school; ask your IDEA contact).
2. Open the browser and go to the Console address your IDEA contact gives you. <!-- screenshot:CON-01 -->
3. In the left menu, click **Network**. <!-- screenshot:CON-02 -->
4. Click the disk that holds your app (or choose **All apps**). <!-- screenshot:CON-03 -->
5. Click the app row, for example Kolibri or Nextcloud. <!-- screenshot:CON-04 -->
6. Look at the status. If it says **Running**, click **Open**. The app opens in a new tab. <!-- screenshot:CON-05 -->

Important:
- Only click **Open** when the app says **Running**.
- If the app is not Running, do **not** press Start or anything else. Tell your IDEA contact or the school operator.
- Do not type numbers (ports) into the address bar. Always use **Open** in the Console.

Tip: after the first time, you can bookmark the page that opens. If the bookmark stops working, go back to the Console and use **Open** again.
