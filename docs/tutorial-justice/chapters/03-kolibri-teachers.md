# 3. Kolibri for teachers

Demo: account `demo-teacher1`, channel Khan Academy (English, US), topic Math by grade > 5th grade > Add and subtract fractions.

## K1. Create a class
1. Open Kolibri from the Console (chapter 2) and sign in with your username and password. <!-- screenshot:K1-01 -->
2. Click **Classes** in the left sidebar. (Exact menu path in our Kolibri version: **to confirm**; it may be under **Coach** or **Facility**.) <!-- screenshot:K1-02 -->
3. Click **+ New class**, type the name (`demo-Grade5A`), click **Save**. <!-- screenshot:K1-03 -->
4. The class appears in the list. It is empty for now.

## K2. Enroll learners
1. Open your class by clicking its name.
2. Click the **Learners** tab, then **Enroll learners**. <!-- screenshot:K2-01 -->
3. Tick each learner (`demo-learner3`), click **Confirm**. <!-- screenshot:K2-02 -->
4. The learners are now in your class and will see what you assign.
Note: learner accounts are created by the school's Kolibri admin. In this demo they already exist.

## K3. Make a lesson and show it to the class
A good lesson has 3 videos (explain) and 3 exercises (practise).
1. In **Coach**, choose your class, then **Lessons** > **New lesson**. Name it `demo-Fractions lesson`. <!-- screenshot:K3-01 -->
2. Open the lesson, click **Manage resources** (button name **to confirm**). <!-- screenshot:K3-02 -->
3. Browse: Khan Academy > Math by grade > 5th grade > Add and subtract fractions. <!-- screenshot:K3-03 -->
4. Tick 3 videos and 3 exercises. Check the counter shows 6 resources. <!-- screenshot:K3-04 -->
5. Save. Check **Recipients** is your class. <!-- screenshot:K3-05 -->
6. Switch the lesson to **Visible**. Learners can now see it. <!-- screenshot:K3-06 -->

## K4. Make a quiz
Quizzes can only use exercises (not videos).
1. Open your class > **Quizzes** > **+ New quiz**. <!-- screenshot:K4-01 -->
2. Choose exercises from Add and subtract fractions; choose the number of questions. <!-- screenshot:K4-02 -->
3. Name it `demo-Fractions quiz` and save.
4. When the class is ready, switch the quiz to **Active** (wording: Start/Active **to confirm** in our version). <!-- screenshot:K4-03 -->
5. When everyone is done, end/close the quiz.

## K5. See progress (Reports)
Do this after learners have worked (chapter 4).
1. Click **Reports** (or the class overview in Coach, **to confirm**) > your class. <!-- screenshot:K5-01 -->
2. **Lessons** > `demo-Fractions lesson`: who has started, who finished each item. <!-- screenshot:K5-02 -->
3. **Quizzes** > `demo-Fractions quiz`: score per learner and which questions were hard. <!-- screenshot:K5-03 -->
4. Click a learner's name to see their own work. <!-- screenshot:K5-04 -->
Tip: do not chase 100%. Look for the question most learners got wrong and teach it again.
