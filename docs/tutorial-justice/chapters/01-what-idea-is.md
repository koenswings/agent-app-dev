# 1. What IDEA is

**IDEA (Initiative for Digital Education in Africa)** is a charity based in Belgium. It puts offline computer systems in rural schools. Everything works from a small server in the school, without internet.

## What is in the school

- **The IDEA box (Engine):** a small Raspberry Pi computer that runs the school's apps. On the demo box it is called `wizardly-hugle`.
- **Disks:** the box has its own **System Disk**. Extra USB disks can be plugged in: a **Backup Disk** that saves the apps' data (chapter 9) and a **Files Disk** for your own files (chapter 8).
- **The IDEA Wi-Fi:** teachers and learners connect their phones, tablets or laptops to it. You only need a web browser.
- **The Console:** a web page that lists the apps and opens them (chapter 2). Operators also use it to look after disks and backups (chapters 10 and 11).

## The apps

| App | What you use it for | Chapters |
|---|---|---|
| **Kolibri** | Ready-made lessons, videos and exercises (for example Khan Academy). You make classes, lessons and quizzes and see each learner's progress. | 3, 4 |
| **Nextcloud** | Your own files: share worksheets with a class, write documents together, collect homework. | 5, 6, 8 |
| **Kiwix** | Wikipedia and other reference books, offline. | 7 |

## Who does what

- **Teachers** open apps from the Console and use Kolibri, Nextcloud and Kiwix (chapters 2 to 7).
- **Learners** sign in with the accounts the teacher gives them (chapters 4 and 6).
- **The school operator** (with the IDEA contact on the phone) looks after disks, backups and restores (chapters 8 to 11).

## How to use this guide

Each chapter has numbered steps with a screenshot. The screenshots come from the IDEA demo box, using demo accounts whose names start with `demo` (see the appendix). Your school's names and addresses will be different. Text marked **to confirm** still has to be checked by the IDEA team.

> **No internet needed.** Everything in this guide works inside the school. Nothing you do is sent to the internet.
