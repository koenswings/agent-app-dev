### Ghost marker correctness — three conditions (non-negotiable)
1. Reference time = `lastFeed.timestamp` (not `now`)
2. Threshold = `D × 1.005 - milkMl - 0.1` (not `D - milkMl`) — Math.round boundary
3. Result = `Math.ceil(hi / 60_000) * 60_000` — ceil to full minute, never midpoint
