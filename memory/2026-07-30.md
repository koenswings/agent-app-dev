### MilkWise web app bugfixes (v1.1.39 → v1.1.43)

#### Bug reported: timeline showed nonsensical sequence (30ml at 00:42 then 120ml at 00:55)
- **Root cause 1:** `canTakeProgression` treated preferred bottle specially — applied only `stomachReadyAt`, skipping `intakeReadyAt`. Smaller bottles had `intakeReadyAt` applied, which in overfed state pushed them to times earlier than the preferred — creating inversions where 60ml appeared AFTER 120ml on the timeline.
- **Root cause 2:** `maxIdx = prefIdx + 1` was including 150ml (preferred+1) — this is actually CORRECT per design; Koen confirmed preferred+1 is intentional as a recovery bottle. Reverted the attempted "fix" of this.
- **Wrong fix tried (v1.1.40):** Removed all sizes but preferred when allFuture=true. Koen rejected this immediately — it violated the spec. The spec says show the full sequence.
- **Wrong fix tried (v1.1.42):** Clamped smaller bottle readyAtMs to stomachReadyAt(preferred) after the fact. Koen rejected — a clamp is a patch, not a correct formula.
- **Correct fix (v1.1.43):** Removed the special-casing of preferred bottle entirely. The formula `readyAt(X) = max(stomachReadyAt(X), intakeReadyAt(X))` applies uniformly to ALL sizes including preferred. Both constraints are monotonically increasing with bottle size, so the ordering invariant holds automatically — no clamps needed. Physics does the sequencing.

#### Key lesson
Never special-case the preferred bottle. The formula is uniform. If the formula produces inversions, the formula is wrong — fix the formula, don't patch with clamps.
